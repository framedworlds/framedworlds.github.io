Orthodox Church

Onion domes under a winter sky, ready for your brushes.

An original church in the Russian Orthodox style, not any real one. A big onion dome on a tall drum crowns the centre, with two slim bell towers on each side, open belfries, little tented roofs and their own domes, each topped by a three-bar cross. Below, tiers of kokoshnik gables step up towards the dome, and a stepped arched portal opens the porch. The domes are covered in scale shingles, and behind them sits a winter sky with flat stylised clouds and small stars.

This one is all about the domes: gold, blue with gold stars, green, or a mix. White walls and a cold blue sky make them pop. The one in the photos is just my take.
