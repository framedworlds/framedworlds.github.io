Sacred Facades

Andalusian Mosque Facade

A moonlit corner of Al-Andalus for your desk.

An original design in the Andalusian-Maghrebi style, not a copy of any real building. A horseshoe-arch portal sits inside its rectangular alfiz moulding, with studded double doors, twisted columns, latticed side windows, a tiled canopy, a carved star frieze and a minaret on the central axis. In front, a polylobed arch opens onto a night sky with a crescent moon and eight-pointed stars.

Stone, plaster, wood and tiles each have their own texture, so it's a treat to paint. Try warm sandstone and white plaster, green or blue tiles, and a deep night sky with gold stars. The one in the photos is just my take.
