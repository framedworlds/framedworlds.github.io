Norwegian Stave Church

A little piece of medieval Norway for your painting desk.

This is an original stave church in bas-relief. It's inspired by the old wooden churches of Norway, but it isn't a copy of any real one. Shingled roofs stack up in tiers to a tiny belfry and spire, with dragon heads at the gable ends. Down below, a covered gallery runs along a fieldstone base, and a gabled porch shelters a tall arched door with iron straps. Fir trees and mountain slopes fill in the background.

It's made for painting. Wood grain on the walls, shingles on every roof, rough stone at the base: a quick drybrush brings all of it out. Go warm timber, grey weathered wood, or dark and moody with snow on the roofs. The one in the photos is just my take.
