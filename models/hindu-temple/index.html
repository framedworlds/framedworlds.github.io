Sacred Facades

Hindu Temple

A North Indian temple seen through a carved stone gateway.

An original design in the Nagara style, not a copy of any real temple. In front, a torana gateway with thick pillars and a garland-shaped arch frames the view. Through it rises the temple: a curved shikhara tower topped by an amalaka and a kalasha finial, two small corner turrets, a stepped porch roof on pillars, a carved frieze and a flight of steps. The sky is carved as scrolling clouds. No figures or symbols are shown.

Sandstone is the obvious choice: warm ochre and pink with darker washes in the stone courses, and a soft sky behind. The one in the photos is just my take.
